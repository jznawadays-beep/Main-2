==================================================

- True financial target: $10,000,000.
- Freedom is the core end-goal.

Major gameplay paths / endings include:

without making it the central objective of the run.


==================================================
2. GAME STRUCTURE
==================================================

Second Skin is structured around:
- daily actions
- stats
- resources
- player choices
- special tasks
- relationship systems
- education / study
- physical development
- financial progression
- major decisions
- long-term endings

Daily actions are a limited resource.

Actions should be allocated according to the current objective
rather than automatically spreading them across every available
activity.


==================================================
3. ACTION SYSTEM
==================================================

Normal action:
- Costs 1 action.

Super Task:
- Costs 2 actions.

Special Task:
- Higher-effort task.
- Exact results depend on the relevant system.
- Do not invent multipliers or outcomes that have not been
  confirmed in gameplay or the manual.

When an activity has an adaptation/efficiency system, repeated
focused use can improve its efficiency.


==================================================
4. RANK SYSTEM
==================================================

Daily performance receives a rank.

Known ranks include:
- S
- A
- B
- lower ranks

Rank evaluates overall daily performance rather than a single
stat alone.

White is the benchmark.

Important:
- White is not automatically an enemy.
- The goal is to outperform White when pursuing competitive
  objectives.
- A player's individual daily rank and the final comparison
  against White are separate calculations.

Example:
- Ryu and White can both receive A individually.
- White can still receive S in the final comparison if White's
  measurable performance was stronger that day.


==================================================
5. WHITE SYSTEM
==================================================

White functions as the primary benchmark/opponent for performance.

White can spend actions on:
- study
- gym
- diet
- social activities
- other available activities

Social activities may provide little or no measurable progress
once the relevant social stat is already maxed.

The player should compare measurable outcomes rather than assuming
every action has equal value.


==================================================
6. ENERGY SYSTEM
==================================================

Energy is a separate resource from actions.

Dense-Core Shake can provide a major temporary energy benefit.

Known state:
- Consuming 3/4 of a Dense-Core Shake can result in infinite
  energy for the active duration.
- Energy should not be confused with action availability.

When infinite energy is active:
- Energy is no longer the primary bottleneck.
- Actions can still limit what the player can perform.


==================================================
7. DENSE-CORE SHAKE
==================================================

Dense-Core Shake:
- Strong energy/body-condition resource.
- Can produce an infinite-energy state when enough is consumed.
- Can improve body condition when paired with proper diet.

Known observation:
- Repeated use alongside a full proper diet causes body condition
  to move progressively toward its prime.

Do not invent additional effects.


==================================================
8. STUDY SYSTEM
==================================================

Study is an action-based system.

Known interaction:
Study
→ choose Exam Study
→ choose Physics / Chemistry / Maths
→ Confirm

Normal Study:
- Costs 1 action.

Study Super Task:
- Costs 2 actions.

IMPORTANT DISCOVERY:
Study efficiency is NOT static.

If Study is used only occasionally:
- initial study output can be inefficient.

Repeated/focused Study:
- allows adaptation.
- increases Study efficiency.
- makes later Study actions more productive.

Confirmed gameplay observations:
- Study efficiency increased by +3 after sustained focused Study.
- Study efficiency later increased by +2 after another focused
  Study session.
- A chapter was completed after repeatedly spending Study actions.

Strategic implication:
Early actions spent building Study adaptation can increase the
value of later actions spent on difficult/high-value chapters.

Therefore:
- Do not judge Study efficiency from a single early attempt.
- Focused Study can be more valuable than scattered Study.
- When exam time is short, concentrated Study may be preferable
  to spreading actions across unrelated activities.

14. EXAM STUDY STRATEGY
==================================================

General approach:

1. Secure high-return chapters first.
2. Use easy/low-difficulty chapters to build Study momentum
   and adaptation when useful.
3. Learn/relearn concepts quickly.
4. Immediately practice CBSE-style questions.
5. Mark topics:
   🟢 Solid
   🟡 Shaky
    Needs another round
6. Use later days for mixed practice, timed papers and weak-topic
   repair.
7. Final revision should focus on:
   - formulas
   - reactions
   - derivations
   - definitions
   - graphs
   - common question patterns
   - mistakes

Important:
The objective is NOT simply to maximize the number of chapters
touched.

Prioritize marks potential, difficulty, current efficiency and
actual mastery.

17. STUDY SESSION FORMAT
==================================================

Preferred learning loop:

Concept
→ Formula / Method
→ Example
→ User solves
→ Mini-test
→ Mark weak spots

Avoid excessively long explanations of material the player already
understands.

The player's two most important academic skills are:

1. Formula recall
2. Recognizing which formula/method applies to a question

Question practice should emphasize these skills.
